keel // a treasury that is a position

Tax does not go to a wallet.
It goes into a position.

Every buy and sell pays 2%, and the contract levers it into a 3× BNB long it holds itself. So the treasury moves on days nobody trades. It settles every five minutes on its own, and 70% of each gain reaches holders as WBNB. No operator account, no published NAV, no pause.

the argument // where tax goes

Tax has to go somewhere

Most of it reaches a wallet, and what happens next is off-chain and unverifiable. This tax becomes a 3× BNB long on Venus that the contract borrows and holds itself — not a buyback, not stablecoins sitting still.

the loop // one job per wake

It settles itself, and only does one thing at a time

No keeper account. Flap's trigger service wakes the vault, and one wake does one job — the callback ceiling is 2,000,000 gas and a build already spends 1.2 million of it.

what is fixed // constants with no setter

Four numbers nobody can move

ConstantValueWhat it decides
TARGET_LEVERAGE3×Venus's 80% collateral factor makes 3× the ceiling a 1.20 health floor can hold.
MIN_HEALTH_BPS12000Caps the debt a build may take on — this floor, not Venus’s own limit.
PROJECT_SHARE_BPS3000The project's share of a gain; holders take the other 70%, always 2.33× it.
Settlement interval5 minOne hour when the last wake found nothing to do.

But the implementation itself can be replaced

The vault runs behind a beacon proxy owned by Flap's Guardian, and that upgrade path is the emergency mechanism — it is why the vault ships no emergency-withdraw function. An upgrade can change every constant above, add a pause, or add a withdrawal. The project's largest trust assumption, and not ours to remove.
