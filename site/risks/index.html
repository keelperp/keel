the list // worst first

Everything that can hurt you

A 16.7% move liquidates the position

the lending market sells the collateral; the treasury is permanently smaller.

Pons's Guardian can replace the implementation

That beacon upgrade is the emergency mechanism, so the vault ships no emergency-withdraw of its own.

Trust moved, not removed

the lending market lends, Uniswap v4 trades, Pons's service wakes; any failure reaches the treasury. If it stops, anyone may restart the chain — nobody is obliged to, and a failed trigger is recorded as failed.

Settlement costs

0.0002 NVDA per wake, paid out of the treasury whether or not there was work — a token nobody trades spends its own treasury staying awake; an empty wake books the next an hour out, not five minutes.

Leverage decays

A choppy market bleeds a 3× position even if NVDA ends flat; carry alone is −4.87% a year.

Dividends need 10,000 KEEL

Below that, no share. Pons's dividend contract sets the floor, not this vault.

No third-party security audit

Submitted to Pons for review and checked rule by rule against their specification. That is a compliance review, not a security audit, and no third party has audited this code.
